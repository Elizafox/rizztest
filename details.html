The Rizz Alignment Framework

(Officially Unofficial Documentation)

What this is (and is not)

The Rizz Alignment Framework is a five-axis interpretive model for describing how someone comes across rather than who they are.

It does not measure morality, intelligence, attractiveness, or worth. It measures presentation, timing, restraint, and the side effects of being perceived.

All results are contingent.
All interpretations are unstable.
Repeated observation affects the system.

The Five Axes

Each axis represents a tension, not a judgement. Movement along an axis is not inherently good or bad; it simply changes how actions are read by others.

V — Authentic ↔ Performative

This axis describes where behaviour appears to originate.

  • Authentic behaviour reads as internally motivated. It would likely occur even without an audience.
  • Performative behaviour appears shaped by anticipation of reaction, approval, or narrative payoff.

High performativity is not lying.
Low authenticity is not dishonesty.
This axis is about source, not truth.

W — Harmless ↔ Powerful

This axis describes perceived force.

  • Harmless presence diffuses tension, avoids dominance, and minimises impact.
  • Powerful presence exerts gravity; people adjust around it, consciously or not.

Power here is not aggression.
Harmlessness is not weakness.
This axis measures how much a person moves the room.

X — Cringe ↔ Based

This axis describes alignment between intent, execution, and reception.

  • Cringe emerges when there is visible mismatch or overreach.
  • Based emerges when action lands cleanly within context.

Cringe is not failure.
Based is not correctness.
This axis is extremely time- and audience-dependent.

Y — Effortless ↔ Tryhard

This axis describes perceived friction.

  • Effortless actions appear low-cost and well-timed.
  • Tryhard actions appear laboured, strained, or over-invested.

High effort is not bad.
Low effort is not lazy.
This axis measures whether the effort shows.

Z — Unhinged ↔ Calculated

This axis describes predictability.

  • Unhinged behaviour feels spontaneous, volatile, or loosely constrained.
  • Calculated behaviour feels planned, paced, and intentional.

Unhinged does not mean chaotic.
Calculated does not mean manipulative.
This axis reflects how visible the planning is.

About Aura

Aura is not a score. Aura is a derivative phenomenon.

It emerges from:

  • The shape of your alignment
  • How often you check it
  • How recently you were last observed
  • Whether you stop touching it

Aura improves when left alone.
Aura degrades when optimised.
This is not a bug.

On Observation and Re-Measurement

Re-taking the test does not invalidate previous results. It adds context.

Repeated checking introduces noise.
Repeated comparison introduces drift.
Obsessing over position destabilises the reading.

If your aura worsens after repeated engagement, that is working as designed.

On Visualisation

Any visual representation of the alignment exists in a projected space.

  • The underlying system is five-dimensional.
  • All projections distort something.
  • Rotation does not change the data, only your perspective.

No single view is canonical.

What Your Result Means (and Doesn’t)

Your result means exactly what it says it means.
No more. No less.

It describes how a set of tendencies read when interpreted together, at a specific moment, under observation.

It does not describe who you are.
It does not describe who you should be.
It does not imply improvement, failure, or trajectory.

If you are looking for a verdict, there isn’t one.
If you are looking for a mirror, you already found it.

The result also means nothing.

Not because it is random, but because it is not authoritative.
It has no power unless you grant it some.

If you feel accurately described, that’s interesting.
If you feel misread, that’s also interesting.
If you feel the urge to argue with it, that reaction is part of the reading.

If you want to know what it “really” means, notice which parts you immediately accepted and which parts you rejected.

Final Notes

This framework is descriptive, not prescriptive.
It is not advice; medical, legal, or any other advice.
It is not diagnosis.
It is not self-help.

Do not attempt to maximise any axis.
Do not attempt to “fix” your result.
Do not attempt to farm aura.

The system has already noticed.
